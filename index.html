Project numbers

Charlie’s 386 homes and 14,900 SF of amenities come from LCOR’s planned program. One Jones Park’s 75 homes and Columbus House’s 60-home mix are published in SERHANT.’s listing material. The case panels link to the supporting pages and marketing examples.

Brooklyn Point’s 100-plus unit sales are historical company reporting from December 2021. Quay Tower’s 90%-plus sold milestone is the developers’ September 2024 cumulative project status. 200 Amsterdam’s 112 residences are confirmed by SJP Properties. None of these figures represents a promised Holland Park result.

Nearby properties & rents

All six rent snapshots link to the properties’ own leasing pages. Hudson House figures use the official Total Rent before promotional concessions. Unit identifiers and fee bases appear in each detail card. These are dated asking-price snapshots, not a guarantee of continuing availability.

Developer context comes from official project portfolios. Legal title and ownership percentages are not independently certified. Embankment’s original developer remains unconfirmed on the official leasing site.

What we have kept out

Unexplained traffic-uplift figures from older sales collateral; conflicting Brooklyn Point unit totals; concession-adjusted headline rents and unconfirmed ownership claims; a Holland Park unit count, final entitlement or delivery date; and claims that every sale or creative asset was delivered by SERHANT. alone.

Nearby-building research is market context. Those six properties are not presented as SERHANT. development assignments. Historical developer announcements are not an independent confirmation of current legal ownership.
