The Vision

A neighborhood arrives between two cities.

Holland Park sits on the last great connective parcel on the Jersey City–Hoboken line. What rises here will not read as a building with an address. It will read as the place where the two cities finally meet.

Holland Park
Public plaza & light rail
The ground floor of a district

A plaza where a rail line becomes a front door.

An elevated Hudson–Bergen Light Rail station, funded by the developer, lands directly into public space.

Districts are made at eye level.

Cafés with tables outside. Shade, steps, planting, a reason to stop. The public realm here is designed to be used before it is admired, and that is exactly how a neighborhood earns its name.

And the ground floor does the introducing.

Retail frontage, a community room, a plaza that reads as a destination from the street. Long before the first residence is leased, this is what makes people say they were at Holland Park.

Chapter one

Not another apartment building. A new part of the city.

02

Lincoln’s Vision

Lincoln Equities is building the connection, not just the address.

Two buildings on either side of a north–south pedestrian plaza. A four-storey podium carrying parking, amenity, retail and community space. And a new light rail station stitched into the plaza, with pedestrian connections out to 18th Street and Grove Street. This is civic infrastructure delivered by a private developer, and it is the reason Holland Park reads as a district.

The plan already tells the story.

A pedestrian and bike crossing to the north. An Erie Street connection to the south. A station in the middle. Lincoln’s drawings describe movement first and units second, and that is the strongest possible foundation for the way this project should be brought to market.

03 · Resident Experience

The commute begins downstairs.

A day that never needs a car.

Step out, cross the plaza, board the light rail. One stop to 2nd Street, a short ride to Hoboken Terminal and Newport, and the PATH into Manhattan from there. Come home to shade, steps, and a street that is busy in the right way.

The feeling we are selling

You do not move into a building here. You move into the neighborhood it created.
